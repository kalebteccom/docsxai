# Interactive viewer UX audit

Scope: the HTML viewer that `docsxai render` (`docsxai-viewer build`) emits, built from `packages/viewer/src/render.ts`, `viewer-markup.ts`, `viewer-style.ts`, `viewer-keys.ts` and the inlined `overlay-runtime.ts`. The burned PNGs (`burn.ts`, `pack`) and the Starlight emitter are out of scope; their pixels and output are unchanged.

The audit was done by reading the source. No browser, screenshot or screen-reader run was part of it. Contrast ratios are computed with the WCAG 2.x relative-luminance formula, and `packages/viewer/test/viewer-style.test.ts` recomputes them from the token objects.

## Findings

Severity: high blocks a task for some readers, medium makes it hard, low is polish.

## Open items

- **Footer timestamp.** Every page carries `Rendered <time>`, so two renders of the same doc pack differ in that line. The rest of the markup is a pure function of the doc pack. Making the line optional, or taking the time from `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH`, is a separate change.
- **Burned badge contrast.** The burner still draws white numbers on `#e8590c` (3.58:1). The burned PNGs were left byte-identical on purpose, so the viewer badge and the burned badge now differ in shade. Changing the burner means new pack hashes for every annotated step.
- **Halo on mid-tone screenshots.** The `#e8590c` halo is 3.58:1 on white and 5.87:1 on black, but no single colour holds 3:1 against every screenshot. The two-tone focus ring covers keyboard focus; the halo itself is unchanged.
- **Retry on `file://`.** The query-string reload works in Chromium and Firefox for `file://` URLs as far as I know. Not checked in Safari.

## Keyboard-only walkthrough

On a rendered pack with at least two flows, with the mouse untouched. `examples/ci/sample-repo/docs-workspace` has one flow, so it covers every step but 6.

1. Open `index.html`. Press Tab once: "Skip to content" appears. Enter moves focus to the main area.
2. Press `→`: focus lands on the first flow card. `→` and `←` move between cards, Home and End jump to the first and last. Enter opens the focused flow.
3. On the flow page, press `→`: focus lands on step 1 and a screen reader says "Step 1 of N: &lt;step&gt;". `→`, `←`, Home and End move between steps.
4. Press Tab from a step: focus reaches the step's call-out halos in order, each read by its copy, and the callout appears. Esc hides it and focus stays.
5. Tab on to "Step write-up", Enter opens it.
6. Press `]` to open the next flow and `[` to come back. Alt+← still goes back in history.
7. Shift+Tab and Tab move out of every element; nothing traps focus.

## Checks that need a browser

These are not covered by the unit tests and were not run:

- The callout shows on focus, on tap in iOS Safari, and hides on Esc.
- Live-region announcements in VoiceOver and NVDA, and whether the `role="alert"` line is announced when it is unhidden.
- No layout shift on load with the reserved image boxes (a CLS reading).
- Overlay redraw after rotation on a phone.
- Print preview with an open and a closed write-up.
- Dark scheme rendering of every page part.
